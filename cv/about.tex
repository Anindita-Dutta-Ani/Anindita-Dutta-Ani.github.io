\section{About Me}

I recently graduated with a \textbf{B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering} from \href{https://www.bracu.ac.bd/}{BRAC University}, Bangladesh, with a CGPA of \textbf{3.58/4.00}. I have a strong foundation in technology and analytical problem-solving, and I am especially drawn to \textbf{Data Science, Deep Learning, Diffusion Models, Graph Neural Networks, and Computer Vision}, with a particular curiosity for how these tools can be applied to biomedical signals and real-world data. For my undergraduate thesis, \textit{Diffusion Models with Graph Attention for Spatiotemporal EEG Inpainting}, I built a hybrid architecture combining \textbf{Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM), Graph Attention Networks (GAT), and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)} to reconstruct missing multichannel EEG data, work that went on to receive the \textbf{Best Thesis Award at BRAC University in 2026}.

Alongside my thesis, I co-authored a peer-reviewed paper on \textbf{vision-based fall detection using anatomical keypoints}, accepted at \textbf{ICCIT 2025}. My experience extends beyond research as well: I have mentored students in robotics and AI, taken on operational and analytical work in a business development role, and led financial and organizational efforts as \textbf{Director of Finance} of the BRAC University Computer Club. I am now looking to pursue graduate studies in \textbf{Data Science}, aiming to deepen my technical and analytical skills and contribute to meaningful research in AI.
