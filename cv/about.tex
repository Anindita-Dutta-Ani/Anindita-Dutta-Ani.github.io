\section{About Me}

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from \href{https://www.bracu.ac.bd/}{BRAC University} with a strong interest in \textbf{Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence}. My undergraduate thesis, \textit{Diffusion Models with Graph Attention for Spatiotemporal EEG Inpainting}, explored the reconstruction of missing multichannel EEG segments through a hybrid deep-learning approach combining \textbf{Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM), Graph Attention Networks (GAT), and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)}.

My broader interests include \textbf{data analysis, data visualization, deep learning, diffusion models, graph neural networks, computer vision, statistical reporting, and biomedical signal processing}. I have also worked on vision-based fall detection and customer churn prediction, applying machine learning, feature engineering, and analytical techniques to real-world problems. In addition to research, I have experience with \textbf{Python, SQL, Excel, Tableau, Looker Studio, OpenCV, and GitHub}, along with practical experience in business and data-driven analysis. My fall-detection research was published as a peer-reviewed paper at \textbf{ICCIT 2025}, while my undergraduate thesis received the \textbf{Best Thesis Award from BRAC University in 2026}. I am now seeking opportunities to pursue graduate studies in \textbf{Data Science} and further strengthen my expertise in data-driven research and intelligent systems.
